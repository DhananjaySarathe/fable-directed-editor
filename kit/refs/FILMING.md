# Filming checklist: what editing can't add later (2026-10-10)

Give this to the owner once per new setup. It covers what the reference study (kit/refs/RESEARCH.md) showed we lack in
the footage itself. It names no gear he doesn't have: his takes so far are iPhone video in his rooms (kit/look rooms).

## Screen and proof (the biggest gap)
- Record the screen for every workflow you talk about (macOS: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording,
  allow the recorder once). Native resolution, the cursor visible, notifications off.
- Show the **real result** and hold it about 3 s without moving the mouse: the PR, the answer, the cost, the file.
  A video that promises "3 ideas" needs the 3 ideas on screen (or a screenshot of them).
- Make text big before recording (browser zoom 125–150 %, editor font 18+). We can zoom 2x in edit; we can't make
  12 px text readable.
- Close private tabs and accounts; we blur what's left, but less is better.
- Keep the failure or the retry if it teaches something: record it, we decide later.

## Face and light
- Key light from the side the camera is on, slightly above eye level; a window works. Avoid a bright window behind you.
- Same light, same spot, same white balance for every take of one video (a lighting change between cuts can't be
  hidden, only covered).
- Eyes about a third from the top of the frame; leave space at the sides for graphics in 16:9.
- Lock exposure and focus on your face (iPhone: press and hold on the face until AE/AF LOCK).

## Sound
- A small room with soft things in it beats a big empty office (the office echo needed dereverb on ai-workflows).
- Record 5 s of silence in the room before you talk (room tone for gaps).
- Phone close and steady; no fan or AC noise if you can switch it off.

## Extra shots that make transitions (optional, 30 s each)
- Your hands typing, the laptop lid closing, the phone lighting up with a message: 2–3 short inserts per build video,
  each 3–5 s, moving in one direction (Daniel Schiffer's matched-move idea: we cut on the motion).
